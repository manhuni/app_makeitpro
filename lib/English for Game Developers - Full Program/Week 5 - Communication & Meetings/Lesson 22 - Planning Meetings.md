## 🗣️ Câu mẫu thực tế

### A. Mời họp (Scheduling)

* Can we **schedule a meeting** for tomorrow afternoon?
* Are you **available** at 3 PM?
* Let’s find a suitable **time slot** for everyone.

### B. Dời / hủy họp

* I need to **reschedule** our meeting to Thursday.
* Unfortunately, I have to **cancel** today's meeting due to a conflict.

### C. Gửi agenda / follow-up

* I’ve attached the **agenda** to this email.
* I’ll send a **follow-up email** after the meeting.

### D. Xác nhận / đồng ý

* Sounds good, I’ll mark it on my **calendar**.
* I’m available at that time.

## ✍️ Bài luyện viết (Writing Practice)

> Viết email mời họp:

**Mẫu:**

```
Subject: Meeting Invitation – Game UI Feedback

Hi team,

I'd like to schedule a meeting to discuss feedback on the latest UI update.

📅 Date: Thursday, June 27  
⏰ Time: 10:00 – 10:30 AM  
📍 Location: Meeting Room 2 / Google Meet

Please let me know if this time works for everyone.  
Best regards,  
[Your Name]
```

## 🎤 Bài luyện nói (Speaking Practice)

> Giả định bạn cần nói trong nhóm qua Zoom:

**Tình huống:** Bạn đề xuất họp để lên kế hoạch bản cập nhật tiếp theo.

```
“Hi everyone, I’d like to propose a quick meeting to align on the next game update.  
How does Friday morning sound to you?  
We can go over the main features and timeline.”  
```

## 🧠 Ghi chú mở rộng

### 🔹 Mẫu cấu trúc lời mời họp (Meeting invitation format):

* **What:** (meeting topic)
* **When:** (date & time)
* **Where:** (platform/location)
* **Why:** (purpose)
* **Who:** (attendees)

### 🔹 Mẫu hội thoại ngắn khi sắp xếp lịch:

**A:** “Are you free around 2 PM tomorrow?”
**B:** “I’m in a meeting until 2:30, can we do 3 PM?”
**A:** “Sure, I’ll send a calendar invite.”
