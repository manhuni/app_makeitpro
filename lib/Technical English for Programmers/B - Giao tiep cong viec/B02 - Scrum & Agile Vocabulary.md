# B02 - Scrum & Agile Vocabulary

## 🎯 Lesson Objective

Learn the essential English vocabulary and expressions used in **Agile software development**, especially within **Scrum** teams, including pronunciation, Vietnamese meanings, and real-world usage.

---

## 🚀 What Is Agile?

**Agile** is a methodology focused on collaboration, adaptability, and delivering value early and often.

**Scrum** is a framework used to implement Agile, featuring roles, events, and artifacts to manage iterative development.

## ✍️ Useful English Phrases in Scrum

| English Phrase                                | Vietnamese Translation                       |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Let's discuss this in the **daily stand-up**. | Hãy thảo luận điều này trong họp hằng ngày.  |
| This story needs a proper **estimation**.     | Câu chuyện này cần được ước lượng chính xác. |
| The **velocity** last sprint was 40 points.   | Tốc độ của sprint trước là 40 điểm.          |
| We need to refine the **product backlog**.    | Chúng ta cần làm rõ lại danh sách công việc. |
| What went well in this **retrospective**?     | Điều gì đã làm tốt trong buổi đánh giá này?  |

## 🔁 Typical Scrum Flow

1. **Product Owner** prioritizes the **backlog**
2. Team holds **Sprint Planning** to select user stories
3. Work progresses through the **Sprint**
4. Daily **Stand-ups** keep team aligned
5. Sprint ends with a **Review** and **Retrospective**

---

## 🗣️ Speaking Practice

Say these aloud:

* “We didn’t finish all our stories this sprint.”
* “Let’s carry that task to the next iteration.”
* “The Product Owner added a new story to the backlog.”

5. The \_\_\_\_\_\_ defines when a task is truly finished.
