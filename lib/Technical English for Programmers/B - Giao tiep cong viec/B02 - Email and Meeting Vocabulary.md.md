# B02 – Email and Meeting Vocabulary

## 🎯 Lesson Objective

Build confidence in using clear and professional English in emails and meetings. Learn essential vocabulary and expressions for asking questions, giving updates, making suggestions, and requesting actions.

## 🧠 Common Email Structures

### 📨 Opening

* “Hope you're doing well.”
* “Just a quick note regarding…”
* “I’m reaching out to…”

### 🧾 Giving Updates

* “Just to keep you in the loop…”
* “Here’s a quick update on…”
* “We’ve made the following progress…”

### 🙋 Asking for Clarification / Info

* “Could you clarify what you meant by…?”
* “Would you mind explaining…?”
* “Just to confirm, are we going with option A or B?”

### ✅ Confirming or Agreeing

* “That sounds good to me.”
* “I’m on board with this plan.”
* “Confirmed, I’ll handle that.”

### 📌 Making a Request

* “Could you take a look at…?”
* “Would you mind reviewing…?”
* “Please let me know your availability.”

### 🎯 Closing

* “Looking forward to your response.”
* “Let me know if you have any questions.”
* “Best regards, \[Your Name]”

---

## 🗣️ Meeting Phrases by Function

### Starting the Meeting

* “Let’s get started.”
* “Thanks for joining.”
* “Here’s the agenda for today…”

### Asking Questions

* “Can I ask a quick question?”
* “What’s the status of…?”
* “Can you elaborate on that point?”

### Clarifying

* “Sorry, can you repeat that?”
* “Do you mean…?”
* “Just to clarify…”

### Giving Updates

* “We’ve completed the frontend work.”
* “Testing is still in progress.”
* “We’re waiting for client feedback.”

### Ending the Meeting

* “Let’s wrap up.”
* “To summarize…”
* “Next steps are…”

## 📧 Example Email

```text
Subject: Update on Sprint 14 Tasks

Hi team,

Just a quick update on the Sprint 14 items:

- The login bug has been fixed and deployed.
- We're still waiting on the API for the notifications panel.
- I’ve scheduled the code review for Friday at 10 AM.

Please let me know if you have any concerns.

Best regards,  
Huy
```

## 🧑‍💻 Example Meeting Excerpt

> **PM:** “Let’s start with a quick status update. Linh, can you go first?”
> **Dev:** “Sure. The profile page refactor is done, and I’ll start integration testing tomorrow.”
> **PM:** “Great. Any blockers?”
> **Dev:** “None at the moment.”

## 📝 Mini Quiz

Choose the most professional phrase:

1. “What do you want?”
   ✅ “Could you clarify what you're asking?”

2. “Tell me again.”
   ✅ “Could you repeat that, please?”

3. “Is it done yet?”
   ✅ “Just checking in — has this task been completed?”
