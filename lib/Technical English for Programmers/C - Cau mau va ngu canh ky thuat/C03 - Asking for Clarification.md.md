# C03 – Asking for Clarification

## 🎯 Lesson Objective

Learn how to politely ask for clarification when something is unclear in meetings, documentation, or code reviews. Build confidence using **natural English expressions** to avoid misunderstanding in technical conversations.

## 🗣️ Useful Phrases – Asking for Clarification

### 👂 When you didn’t understand something:

* “Sorry, I didn’t quite catch that. Could you repeat it?”
* “I’m not sure I understand what you mean by *X*.”
* “Could you clarify what you mean by that?”
* “Just to be clear, are you saying that…?”
* “Could you walk me through that part again?”

### 🧠 When something is ambiguous or vague:

* “There are a few possible interpretations. Could you be more specific?”
* “I’m a bit confused about this requirement. Can we go over it again?”
* “Do you mean X, or are you referring to Y?”

### 💡 When paraphrasing to confirm understanding:

* “So, just to confirm, you mean that we should…?”
* “If I understand correctly, the feature should behave like this: …”
* “Let me repeat that to make sure I got it…”

## 🤖 In Technical Context

| Scenario                         | Example Phrase                                                    |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Unclear requirement              | “What exactly does ‘optimize’ mean in this context?”              |
| Ambiguous comment in code review | “Could you explain what you mean by ‘too complex’ here?”          |
| Confusing error message or log   | “This log isn’t clear to me. What does ‘ERR\_CODE\_42’ refer to?” |
| Vague feedback from stakeholder  | “When you say ‘more user-friendly’, do you have an example?”      |

---

## 🔁 Asking for Clarification Politely

| Weak (Too vague)           | Improved (Polite & Specific)                                  |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| “What?”                    | “Sorry, I didn’t catch that. Could you repeat it, please?”    |
| “I don’t get it.”          | “I’m not sure I follow. Can you explain that in another way?” |
| “That doesn’t make sense.” | “Could you clarify that part a bit more?”                     |

---

## 🧑‍💻 Example Dialogue

**You:**

> “Sorry, I didn’t quite follow that last part. Are we supposed to refactor the entire module, or just this function?”

**Teammate:**

> “Just the function. The rest can stay as it is.”

**You:**

> “Got it. Thanks for the clarification.”

## 📝 Mini Practice

**Rewrite these vague questions into professional English:**

1. “What does that mean?” →
   ✅ “Could you explain what you mean by that?”

2. “Say that again.” →
   ✅ “Sorry, I missed that. Would you mind repeating it?”

3. “I don’t get what you want.” →
   ✅ “Could you clarify what the goal is for this task?”
