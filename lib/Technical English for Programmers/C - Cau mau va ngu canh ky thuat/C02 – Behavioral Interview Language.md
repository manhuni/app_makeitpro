# C02 – Behavioral Interview Language

## 🎯 Lesson Objective

Learn how to confidently answer **behavioral interview questions** using English expressions and frameworks like **STAR** (Situation, Task, Action, Result). Improve fluency in explaining teamwork, conflict resolution, ownership, and communication experiences.

## 💬 STAR Technique Overview

Use the **STAR** format to structure your answers:

* **S** – Situation: What was the context?
* **T** – Task: What was your responsibility?
* **A** – Action: What did you do?
* **R** – Result: What was the outcome?

---

## 🗣️ Useful Sentence Starters

### ⭐ Situation / Task

* “In my previous project at \[company], we faced a challenge when…”
* “I was responsible for…”
* “Our team needed to deliver a feature under a tight deadline.”

### 🛠 Action

* “I took initiative by…”
* “I collaborated with...”
* “I suggested we...”
* “I broke the task into smaller parts and prioritized them.”

### ✅ Result

* “As a result, we launched on time and received positive feedback.”
* “The issue was resolved without further escalation.”
* “It improved performance by 30%.”

---

## 🧑‍💼 Sample Behavioral Questions & Answers

### 🔸 Question 1: “Tell me about a time you faced a conflict in a team.”

**Answer (STAR format):**

> In a sprint planning meeting (S), a teammate and I disagreed on the solution to a bug. I was tasked with leading the fix (T). I initiated a one-on-one chat to better understand their concerns and proposed a compromise that combined both ideas (A). We resolved the conflict and shipped the fix without delay (R).

---

### 🔸 Question 2: “Describe a time when you took initiative.”

**Answer:**

> Our project was delayed due to lack of testing automation (S). Although it wasn’t assigned to me (T), I built a small CI/CD script to automate tests using GitHub Actions (A). This saved hours of manual testing each week (R).

---

### 🔸 Question 3: “How do you handle pressure or tight deadlines?”

**Answer:**

> When facing tight deadlines, I first clarify the scope with stakeholders (A), prioritize tasks using the Eisenhower Matrix (A), and communicate clearly with the team if trade-offs are needed. This approach helps avoid burnout and ensures delivery (R).
