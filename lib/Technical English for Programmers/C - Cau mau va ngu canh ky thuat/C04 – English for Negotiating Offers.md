# C04 – English for Negotiating Offers

## 🎯 Lesson Objective

Learn how to **negotiate job offers** professionally in English, including salary, benefits, and other terms. Build confidence in expressing your expectations, asking questions, and responding to offers respectfully.

## 💬 Useful Phrases for Negotiation

### 🗣 Expressing Interest (khẳng định sự quan tâm)

* “I’m really excited about this opportunity.”
* “This role is a great match for my skills.”
* “I’d love to be part of the team.”

### 💸 Asking About Salary

* “Could we talk about the compensation package?”
* “Is the salary negotiable?”
* “Based on my experience, I was expecting something in the range of \[\$X–\$Y].”

### 🤝 Making a Counter-Offer

* “Thank you for the offer. Is there flexibility on the base salary?”
* “I’d be more comfortable if the salary was closer to \[\$X].”
* “Would you consider adjusting the equity component?”

### 📦 Asking About Benefits

* “Can you share more about the benefits package?”
* “Does the company support remote work or flexible hours?”
* “Is there a learning or development budget available?”

---

## 📘 Sample Dialogue

**You:**

> “Thank you for the offer. I’m really excited about the role. I had a question about the base salary. Based on industry benchmarks and my background, would you be open to discussing a slightly higher number?”

**Recruiter:**

> “We might have some flexibility. What range are you thinking of?”

**You:**

> “I was hoping for something closer to \$3,000 per month, given my 3 years of experience and full-stack background.”

---

## 📋 Tips for Negotiating Respectfully

✅ Be polite but assertive
✅ Show appreciation before asking
✅ Justify your request (experience, market rate, previous offers)
✅ Be honest and clear
✅ Always get the final offer in writing

---

## 🚫 What to Avoid Saying

| Don’t Say...                       | Better Alternative                                |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| “That’s too low.”                  | “Is there any flexibility on the number?”         |
| “I need more money.”               | “Based on market research, I was expecting more.” |
| “Company A offered more.” (rudely) | “I’ve received other offers in the range of…”     |

## 📝 Mini Quiz

**Choose the best way to express each intention:**

1. You want to ask about benefits →
   a) “Do you guys give good stuff?”
   b) “Could you tell me more about the benefits package?” ✅

2. You want to counter the base salary →
   a) “This pay is bad.”
   b) “Would you consider adjusting the base salary?” ✅

3. You want to stay polite →
   a) “That’s too low for me.”
   b) “Thank you. Is there any room to negotiate?” ✅
