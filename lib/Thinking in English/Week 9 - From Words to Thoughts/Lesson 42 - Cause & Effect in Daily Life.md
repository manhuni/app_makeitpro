 

# ⚙️ **Lesson 42 – Cause & Effect in Daily Life**

> 🎯 **Mục tiêu bài học:**

* Hiểu và sử dụng cấu trúc **nguyên nhân – kết quả** (Cause & Effect)
* Ứng dụng vào giao tiếp đời sống
* Phát triển kỹ năng nói – viết logic và mạch lạc hơn

---

## 📘 **1. Từ vựng chính (Vocabulary + IPA)**

| Từ/Cụm từ   | IPA            | Nghĩa                    | Ví dụ câu                                              |
| ----------- | -------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| because     | /bɪˈkəz/       | bởi vì                   | I was late **because** of traffic.                     |
| so          | /səʊ/          | vì vậy / nên             | It rained, **so** I stayed home.                       |
| therefore   | /ˈðeə.fɔːr/    | do đó                    | He was tired, **therefore** he slept early.            |
| as a result | /əz ə rɪˈzʌlt/ | kết quả là               | She forgot her umbrella. **As a result**, she got wet. |
| due to      | /ˈdjuː tuː/    | bởi vì (trang trọng hơn) | The flight was delayed **due to** bad weather.         |

---

## 💬 **2. Câu mẫu + Kịch bản ngắn**

### ✅ **Câu mẫu (Cause → Effect):**

* I didn’t eat breakfast, **so** I was hungry.
* She was sick **because** she played in the rain.
* **Due to** the storm, the game was canceled.
* He lost his keys, **therefore** he couldn’t enter the house.
* I missed the bus. **As a result**, I was late.

---

### 🎭 **Kịch bản ngắn – Mối quan hệ nhân quả**

> It rained heavily this morning, **so** the roads were flooded.
> **As a result**, many people arrived late at work.
> Tom forgot his umbrella **because** he was in a hurry.
> **Therefore**, he got completely wet.

---

## 🧠 **3. Bài luyện nói tư duy (Think & Speak)**

🎯 Hãy nhìn vào các tình huống và trả lời bằng câu **nguyên nhân – kết quả**:

1. You didn’t sleep well last night. What happened today?
   → I was tired **because** I didn’t sleep well.

2. You got a high score on your test. Why?
   → I studied hard, **so** I got a high score.

3. It rained yesterday. What did you do?
   → It rained, **so** I stayed home.

4. The power went out. What was the effect?
   → The power went out. **As a result**, I couldn’t use my computer.

## ✍️ **4. Bài luyện viết phản xạ (Quick Writing Practice)**

### 📝 Viết 5 câu thể hiện **mối quan hệ nguyên nhân – kết quả**:

**Gợi ý mẫu:**

1. I didn’t eat lunch, **so** I was very hungry.
2. He missed the train **because** he woke up late.
3. It was cold, **so** I wore a jacket.
4. She worked hard, **therefore** she got a promotion.
5. The internet was slow. **As a result**, I couldn’t watch the video.

---

## 📎 **5. Ghi chú thêm (Ngữ pháp)**

### 🔍 **Các cách phổ biến để thể hiện Cause & Effect**

#### ✅ **1. Using “because”**

* **Cấu trúc:** Result + **because** + Cause
* I stayed home **because** I was sick.

#### ✅ **2. Using “so”**

* **Cấu trúc:** Cause + **so** + Result
* It was raining, **so** we canceled the picnic.

#### ✅ **3. Using “due to” / “because of”** (giới từ → theo sau là danh từ/cụm danh từ)

* **Cấu trúc:** Result + **due to** + noun
* The road is closed **due to** an accident.

#### ✅ **4. Using “therefore” / “as a result”** (trang trọng, viết học thuật hơn)

* **Cấu trúc:** \[Cause]. **Therefore**, \[Result].
* He didn’t study. **Therefore**, he failed.

---

## 📌 Mẹo học:

* Ghi nhớ:
  👉 "because" + **mệnh đề**
  👉 "because of" / "due to" + **danh từ/cụm danh từ**

* Luyện tạo **bản đồ tư duy nguyên nhân – kết quả** từ các hành động hàng ngày

* Kết hợp các từ nối từ bài trước (**and, but, because**) với **so, therefore, as a result**
