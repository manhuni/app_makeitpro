 

# 🏙️ **Lesson 33 - City & Transportation**

## 💬 **2. Câu mẫu + Kịch bản ngắn**

### ✅ **Câu mẫu (Sample Sentences)**

* I go to work by bus every day.
  /aɪ ɡəʊ tə wɜːk baɪ bʌs ˈevri deɪ/
  → Tôi đi làm bằng xe buýt mỗi ngày.

* There is a lot of traffic in the city.
  /ðeər ɪz ə lɒt əv ˈtræfɪk ɪn ðə ˈsɪti/

* Where is the nearest bus stop?
  /weər ɪz ðə ˈnɪərɪst bʌs stɒp/

* You can cross the street at the traffic light.
  /juː kæn krɒs ðə striːt æt ðə ˈtræfɪk laɪt/

---

### 🎭 **Kịch bản ngắn (Mini-dialogue)**

**A:** How do you get to school?
/hɑʊ duː juː ɡet tə skuːl/

**B:** I ride my bicycle. It’s fast and fun!
/aɪ raɪd maɪ ˈbaɪsɪk(ə)l. ɪts fɑːst ənd fʌn/

**A:** That’s nice! I usually take the bus.
/ðæts naɪs! aɪ ˈjuːʒuəli teɪk ðə bʌs/

## 🧠 **3. Bài luyện nói tư duy (Think & Speak)**

👉 Trả lời ngắn gọn bằng tiếng Anh, luyện phản xạ tư duy:

1. How do you usually go to work or school?
2. What transportation do people use in your city?
3. Do you like taking the subway? Why or why not?
4. What do you do when the traffic is bad?

---

## ✍️ **4. Bài luyện viết phản xạ (Quick Writing Practice)**

### Viết đoạn ngắn (3–5 câu):

**"Describe how you travel in the city."**
(*Miêu tả cách bạn đi lại trong thành phố.*)

🧾 *Ví dụ:*

> I usually take the bus to school. It takes about 20 minutes. Sometimes I walk if the weather is nice. There is a lot of traffic in the morning, so I leave early.

---

## 📎 **5. Ghi chú thêm (Grammar Tips)**

### ✅ Cấu trúc miêu tả phương tiện:

* **S + go(es) + by + phương tiện**
  → I go **by bus**.
  → He goes **by motorbike**.

* **S + take(s) + the + phương tiện**
  → She takes **the train** every day.

* **S + ride(s) + xe đạp/xe máy**
  → I ride a bike. / He rides a motorbike.
