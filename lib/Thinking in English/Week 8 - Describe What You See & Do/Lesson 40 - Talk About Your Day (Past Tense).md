

# 🕒 **Lesson 40 – Talk About Your Day (Past Tense)**

> 🎯 **Mục tiêu bài học:**

* Biết cách mô tả lại những gì đã làm trong ngày
* Ghi nhớ các động từ ở quá khứ
* Luyện kỹ năng nói & viết phản xạ về trải nghiệm hằng ngày

## 💬 **2. Câu mẫu + Kịch bản ngắn**

### ✅ **Câu mẫu (Sample Sentences)**

* I **woke up** at 6 a.m.
* I **went to school** and **studied** math.
* I **ate lunch** with my friends.
* In the evening, I **watched TV** and **did my homework**.
* I **went to bed** at 10 p.m.

---

### 🎭 **Kịch bản ngắn – Kể lại 1 ngày**

> Yesterday was a busy day.
> I woke up at 7 a.m. and had a quick breakfast.
> Then, I went to work and had a meeting with my team.
> After work, I went to the supermarket and bought some food.
> In the evening, I relaxed and watched a movie.
> It was a tiring but good day.

---

## 🧠 **3. Bài luyện nói tư duy (Think & Speak)**

🎯 Trả lời các câu hỏi sau bằng thì quá khứ đơn:

1. What time did you wake up yesterday?
2. What did you eat for lunch?
3. Who did you meet or talk to?
4. What did you do in the afternoon?
5. How did you feel at the end of the day?

> 💡 Gợi ý mở đầu:

* Yesterday I...
* In the morning...
* In the evening...
* After that...

---

## ✍️ **4. Bài luyện viết phản xạ (Quick Writing Practice)**

📝 **Viết một đoạn văn 5–7 câu về ngày hôm qua:**

> *Ví dụ:*

> Yesterday, I woke up early and went jogging in the park.
> I had eggs and bread for breakfast.
> Then I went to school and studied English and science.
> In the afternoon, I played badminton with my friend.
> After dinner, I watched a movie and went to sleep at 10 p.m.
> It was a good day.

---

## 📎 **5. Ghi chú thêm (Ngữ pháp cần biết)**

### 🔧 **Thì Quá Khứ Đơn (Simple Past Tense)**

✅ **Cấu trúc khẳng định:**

* S + V2 (quá khứ đơn)

  * I **visited** my grandmother.

✅ **Câu phủ định:**

* S + did not (didn’t) + V1

  * I **didn’t eat** noodles.

✅ **Câu hỏi:**

* Did + S + V1?

  * **Did** you **go** to school yesterday?

📌 **Chú ý:**

* **Động từ bất quy tắc** cần học thuộc (go → went, do → did, eat → ate...)
* Với động từ **có quy tắc**, thêm **-ed** (play → played, study → studied...)
